Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

This app ("Channel") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (Ask Ogg, Ask Quark, Scalefire, Last Signal Horror) through the YouTube Data API.

The app accesses only the owner's own YouTube account data with the owner's consent. It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other users. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared with third parties.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used; see the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
